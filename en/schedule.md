## 1. Authoritative Fall 2026 timing {#timing}

- The course runs for **15 weeks**, Mondays 14:00–16:30 (Asia/Shanghai), from 14 September through 21 December 2026.
- A photo of the handwritten weekly lecture note is due within 24 hours, by Tuesday 16:30.
- Six individual assessed tasks are released after class in Weeks 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12 and are due exactly seven days later.
- Each task is worth 5 points. The six tasks total 30 points and constitute the Individual Assignments 30% component.
- The timestamps in `learn.spaiq.ai` are authoritative for release, submission, and late status.

## 4. Weekly learning loop {#loop}

Prepare and investigate → model or experiment in class → organize handwritten notes and photograph them → submit the note within 24 hours → complete the current evidence task or project gate → review teacher and AI feedback → verify the next claim with a counterexample or transfer probe.

The [Assignments and Labs]({{ '/en/assignments/' | relative_url }}) page defines the task artifacts and rubric. This page defines the dates; no other page may introduce a different deadline or an additional independently graded assignment.
